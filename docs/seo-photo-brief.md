# SEO Photo Brief

This brief defines the photos Senja Malere should collect before finalizing website imagery or adding project examples. Publish photos only when they are verified, truthful, and safe to show publicly. Service-page imagery belongs to the same verified-photo set; there is no separate category for generic publishable service illustrations. After the photo-finalization stage, images kept on the website are treated as verified publishable assets, not placeholders.

## Highest Priority

- Homepage hero: one strong wide photo of a finished painted home, room, facade, or work scene. It should look calm, bright, and professional.
- Interior painting page: finished wall, room, trim, ceiling, hallway, living room, or bedroom.
- Exterior painting page: facade, siding or kledning, window trim, outdoor detail, or house exterior after painting.
- Furniture painting page: cabinet, door, built-in unit, table, chair, wardrobe, or detail surface.
- Google Business Profile: 5-10 clear real photos showing finished work, work process, tools or materials, and service-area relevance.

## Good Supporting Photos

- Painter at work, ideally without showing faces unless consent is clear.
- Surface preparation: sanding, masking, priming, filling cracks, or similar preparation work.
- Detail closeups: clean edges, trim, corners, doors, cabinets, or painted finish.
- Before-and-after pairs only when both photos are real, from the same project, and clearly comparable.
- Local context shots from Senja or Finnsnes only when they are genuinely relevant and not misleading as project photos.

## Avoid

- Dark or blurry photos.
- Messy unfinished scenes unless they explain the work process clearly.
- Photos of homes where publishing permission is unclear.
- Anything that implies reviews, guarantees, certifications, years of experience, or other business claims that have not been verified.
- Stock-looking photos or decorative images presented as project evidence.

## Format Guidance

- Prefer landscape photos for hero and page image slots.
- Use high-resolution originals where possible.
- Prefer natural light and realistic color.
- Avoid heavy filters, text overlays, watermarks, and screenshots from social media.
- Keep source notes for each photo: what it shows, where it was taken if relevant, what kind of work, process, tool, material, or surface it documents, and whether permission to publish is clear.

## Launch Decision

Finalize page images first. Do not treat finalized website images as placeholders. Add a compact work examples section only if there are at least 4-6 strong verified project photos.
